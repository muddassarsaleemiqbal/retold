# Hindi voice notes

- Everyday spoken Hindi (बोलचाल की हिंदी), the way a good storyteller talks on YouTube, not
  textbook or news Hindi. Avoid heavy Sanskritised words when a common word exists.
- Common English loanwords people actually say are fine (फ़ोन, पुलिस, प्लान); do not force
  pure-Hindi replacements.
- Keep the storyteller register: "वो", "उसे", "लेकिन", "तो", "जब तक". Never "और फिर".
- Character and place names stay as they are spoken in the film, written in Devanagari.
- Numbers and years as spoken words ("उन्नीस सौ बाईस").
- Same rhythm rules as English: short punches mixed with longer lines; the twist alone.
- The closing question addresses the viewer as "आप".
